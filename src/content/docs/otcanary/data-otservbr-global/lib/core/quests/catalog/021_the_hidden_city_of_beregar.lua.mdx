## 📄 Visão Geral do Código

### Resumo Executivo

"The Hidden City of Beregar" é uma **quest de exploração de dungeon-city**:

```
Contexto: Beregar (hidden dwarven city underground)
Objetivo: Ajudar cidadãos e explorar
↓
5 Missões Temáticas:
  1. Going Down: Reparar elevador
  2. Justice for All: Investigar acusação injusta
  3. Pythius the Rotten: Derrotar boss undead
  4. Sweet as Chocolate Cake: Quest culinária
  5. The Good Guard: Conseguir bebida
↓
Resultado: Acesso a diferentes áreas (eastern, northern, western mine)
```

## 8. City Structure via Quest Rewards

```
Main Entrance
  ↓ M1: Elevator

Central City
  ↓ M2: Justice
  ↓ M5: Guard relations
Northern Mine (Grombur area)
  
Central
  ↓ M3: Boss
  ↓ M4: Cooking

```

Cada missão desbloqueia um setor diferente.

## 10. Tipo de Quest: Urban Exploration System

### Características:

- ✅ 5 missões, cada una desbloqueando setor
- ✅ Variety de tasks (engineering, investigation, combat, cooking, fetch)
- ✅ Multi-area exploration
- ✅ NPC relationships matter (Grombur appears twice)
- ✅ City structure progression (locked areas unlock)
- ✅ One-time boss fight (Pythius)
- ✅ Cross-city trading (to Venore)

## 11. Tema: Hidden City Discovery

```
Beregar = Secret dwarven city
Players gradually unlock access:
  - Repair infrastructure (elevator)
  - Solve political issues (justice)
  - Defeat threats (boss)
  - Contribute labor (cooking, drinks)
→ Become trusted citizen of city
```
